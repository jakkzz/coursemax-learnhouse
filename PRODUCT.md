# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Teachers manage assigned courses and inspect their classes and students. Students access their own learning and institutional identity. Authorized administrators review identity disputes and manage classes within their authorized scope.

## Product Purpose

Extend LearnHouse with a coherent institutional teaching and learning experience. Native accounts, sessions, organization navigation and course authoring remain LearnHouse-owned; official school identities, term-specific offerings, rosters and academic evidence have separate bounded authorities.

## Operating Context

Course content is reusable; a class is a term-specific offering/section. Teachers may view every section of an explicitly assigned course, not all courses. Administrators resolve disputed school mappings; teachers flag issues. School mapping includes both students and teachers. The initial UI milestone is a clearly synthetic clickable prototype, not a live verification or roster service.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One native account/session authority. Personal Gmail remains valid; school verification does not replace Google or linked LINE login.
- Provider subject owns login identity. Names and email domains do not merge accounts, prove school identity, grant roles or create enrollment.
- Course assignments, membership, school identity and account linkage remain visibly distinct.
- Manual, unlinked, inactive and withdrawn roster identities must remain representable.
- Preserve immutable academic evidence and reviewed source identities. Real migration and production cutover require separate approval after accepted parity.
- No proactive messaging, real school/person imports or security-policy changes as prototype shortcuts.
- Undecided: school verification method, institutional proof integration and historical-access policy. Prototype evidence must not be represented as real verification.

## Brand Commitments

Inherit LearnHouse's incumbent product UI. This feature is not a rebrand or global redesign. English and natural Thai product copy, calm task-oriented workflows, concise actions and mobile layouts without horizontal overflow are required.

## Evidence on Hand

Incumbent dashboard navigation, Users tables, shared UI controls and account screens in apps/web. Prototype data is explicitly fictional. Existing authentication acceptance does not establish institutional or academic parity.

## Product Principles

- Show the next safe task and resulting state.
- Group courses and sections without conflating content and rosters.
- Separate capability visibility from server authorization.
- Make exceptions actionable and disputes human-reviewed.
- Develop with synthetic evidence; migrate real people and coursework last.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable controls, explicit labels, readable semantic status text and touch-sized actions. Validate teacher, student and administrator task completion at desktop and mobile widths in English and Thai.
